# Exploratory scripts

Scripts written while the results were derived (7 to 8 October 2026). They are
kept for provenance, not polished: paths and outputs are as they were used.
The polished versions of these computations are in `undeRfactor`, `proofs/` and
`simulation/`.

| Scripts | Paper |
|---|---|
| `closed_forms.py`, `global1f.py`, `indep_proof_check.py`, `fullparam*.py`, `reduced_check.py` | Theorem 1: closed forms against full-parameter optimization; many-items limit |
| `thresholds.py`, `table_exact.py`, `cfi_rows*.{py,txt}`, `cfi_check.R`, `other_idx.R`, `srmr_low.R`, `chart_*.json` | Section 5 and Table 1: population fit indices and cutoff thresholds |
| `heywood_check.py`, `check_agent2*.py` | Proposition 2 and the Heywood counterexample of Lemma 1 |
| `beyond*.py`, `fig2_*.{py,json}`, `fold*.py`, `localmin.py`, `favour_sizes.py`, `switch.py`, `checkfav.py`, `bif_rows.txt`, `eps_scaling.json` | Section 3.1: unequal blocks, folds (Proposition 1), cube-root law (Theorem 3), Figure 2 |
| `binary4.py`, `bincheck.py`, `binsym.py` | Section 7.1: exact binary items |
| `strachan.py`, `spec_check.py` | Section 7.2 and Section 6: the Strachan et al. (2022) design, cross-loadings |
| `phqads.py`, `phq_thr.py`, `scope_probe*.py` | Section 8: PHQ-ADS |
| `partitions.py`, `paper_conditions.py`, `bbp.py`, `tefi_check.R`, `sim_select.R` | Side checks: partitions of the items, O-information, spectral detectability, TEFI |
